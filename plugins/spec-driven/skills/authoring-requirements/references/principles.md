# Default principles

Every spec starts with these. A finding one of them decides is settled: cite it (H5, A1) and do not ask. Two that conflict make a question for the operator. They decide behaviour; they never put UI design into a rule.

## Nielsen's usability heuristics, read as rules of behaviour

- **H1 · Visibility of system status.** A person never has to guess whether an action took effect, or what state a thing is in.
- **H2 · Match between the system and the real world.** Product words come from the domain and the existing product. Never invent a term.
- **H3 · User control and freedom.** A person can undo, or leave. Nothing that a person or a record relies on is destroyed: it leaves use instead. The system never reverses a person's choice on its own.
- **H4 · Consistency and standards.** One action behaves the same wherever it appears. Behind a switch, people who did not turn it on see what they saw before.
- **H5 · Error prevention.** Before a step that is hard to reverse, show its consequences. Input with no defined place is refused, not guessed at.
- **H6 · Recognition rather than recall.** Show what the system already knows, such as a thing's current state or placement, instead of asking a person to remember it.
- **H7 · Flexibility and efficiency of use.** A frequent action gets a fast path, such as a bulk action, and the careful path stays.
- **H8 · Aesthetic and minimalist design.** Show only what the decision at hand needs.
- **H9 · Help users recognise, diagnose and recover from errors.** A refusal says why, in plain words, and what to do next.
- **H10 · Help and documentation.** A behaviour that a person can find only by being told about it is a finding.

## Additions for products that many people and processes change

- **A1 · Act on the state the person saw.** An action against a state that changed since the person saw it is refused, or confirmed again with what changed.
- **A2 · Make before break.** A replacement never leaves a gap: the old serves until the new is ready.
- **A3 · Mechanism, not special cases.** Build the general mechanism and let configuration choose the policy. No built-in special value, and no limit of one where the world has many.
